OUR COOPERATIVE

Rested Root Corp is a worker-owned cooperative of Black and Queer educators, facilitators, and organizers. We have nearly 2 decades of combined experience offering liberatory teaching and consulting to schools, universities, nonprofits, community groups, businesses, and governmental organizations.


Meet Our Team

HOW WE WORK

We nurture vulnerable connections and strategic solutions — rooting into the body, stretching in understanding, and transforming oppressive dynamics.

Embody

We begin with grounding meditation & breathplay — rooting into our bodies to deepen the resources we can tap to heal and transform.

Analyze

Historical and political analysis to understand how systemic oppression is manifesting in interpersonal and institutional dynamics.

Connect

Nurturing vulnerable connections and honest dialogue about identity, power, privilege, and oppression.

Transform

Strategic solutions and creative action planning to transform oppressive dynamics into opportunities for growth.
