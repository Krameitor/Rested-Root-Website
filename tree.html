GROWING LIBERATION

Our "Tree of Change" methodology guides the journey from awareness to embodied transformation — a living framework for collective liberation.

Seeds of Awareness

All of us have awareness buried in our consciousness that the current status quo is deeply inequitable, unfair, and unjust. These seeds are the starting point — the recognition that something must change.

Saplings of Understanding

When nourished, these seeds of awareness grow into saplings of understanding — the realization that our society is dominated by a system that values some lives over others. Understanding becomes the foundation for action.

Trunks of Our Truth

We all have a truth that arises from our experience living in an inequitable society. These truths — when spoken, honored, and held collectively — become the sturdy trunks that support the canopy of liberation.

Rooted in Embodiment

When we root into our bodies, we deepen the resources that we can tap to heal and transform the legacies of our ancestral and lived trauma. Embodiment is not a destination — it is an ongoing practice of liberation.
